# UNFNSHD brand identity

Decided by Luke. Logo shape decided 26 October 2026; colours, fonts and app look updated to
**Emerald** on 5 November 2026. This file wins over older notes.

Design references (claude.ai Design canvases):
- "UNFNSHD app in Emerald": every client and coach screen, light and dark. The layouts there
  are the target for the app.
- "UNFNSHD logo in Emerald": lockups, app icon options, logo in use, logo rules.
- "UNFNSHD shortlist pairs" and the big "UNFNSHD colour combos" canvas: the exploration that
  led here (132 combos, kept for reference).

## What the brand means
"We aren't done. We can still work and move." Never content, not never happy. The journey,
not the outcome. The mark shows it: a ring that is not quite closed, with the next step (the
dot) waiting in the gap.

## Logo (unchanged shape)
- **Symbol: the open ring.** A thick ring, about 89% closed, round line ends, gap at the top
  right, a dot sitting in the gap. Small-size SVG (40 x 40 box):
  `<path d="M33 13.5A15 15 0 1 1 26.5 6.4" stroke="INK" stroke-width="5" stroke-linecap="round"/>`
  `<circle cx="32" cy="8" r="3.5" fill="DOT"/>`
- **Name: UNFNSHD, all uppercase, Baloo 2 ExtraBold (800).**
- **Lockup:** ring to the left of the name. A stacked version (ring above name) for square
  spaces. The ring alone for icons and tiny sizes.
- **Colours, only these three ways:** emerald on cream with a tan dot; cream on emerald with a
  tan dot; emerald on tan with a cream dot (the dot is tan everywhere except on tan).
- **Rules:** clear space at least half the ring's height. Full logo at least 24 px tall; below
  that use the ring alone, down to 16 px. Never close the ring, never stretch it, never
  recolour it outside emerald, cream and tan, never yellow on black (the competitor CNDTND's
  look).

## The signature: the ring never closes
Every progress ring in the app is drawn like the logo: an open arc (320 of 360 degrees, gap at
the top right) with a tan dot in the gap. Even a perfect week leaves the ring open.

## Two modes
Light is the default; first launch follows the phone; the client can switch in settings.

### Light mode: Emerald Day
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page background | #F4EFE6 (cream) |
| Cards, sheets, inputs | #FFFFFF |
| Subtle fill (tracks, segmented, quiet rows) | #EBE4D6 |
| Text, rings, primary buttons | #0B2B24 (emerald) |
| Button text on emerald | #F4EFE6 |
| Secondary text | #4A5A53 |
| Faint text | #7C877F |
| Hairlines / input borders | #E3DACA / #D9CFBC |
| Hero blocks (the big card on a screen) | #0B2B24 with cream text |
| Wins, PBs, the dot, avatars | #D6B98C (tan) with emerald text |
| Second ring / soft accent | #7FB8A4 (sage), #E2EEE8 (soft emerald) |

### Dark mode: Emerald Club
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Page background | #0B2B24 (emerald) |
| Cards, sheets | #123A31 |
| Subtle fill | #0F332B |
| Text | #F4EFE6 (cream) |
| Secondary text | #B3C4BB |
| Hairlines / input borders | #1B4639 / #2A5A4B |
| Primary buttons | #D6B98C (tan) with #0B2B24 text |

| Wins, PBs, the dot | #D6B98C |

The two modes swap: in light the hero block is emerald on cream, in dark it is cream on
emerald, and buttons turn tan.

### Status colours (coach screens only; clients never see these labels)
Engaged: soft emerald. Drifting: soft amber (#F3E6CF / #6B4A12; dark #4A3A1E / #F1D9A8).
At risk: soft clay (#F6DDD5 / #8A2E1C; dark #4F2A22 / #F5C6B8). New: neutral.

## Type
- Display: **Baloo 2, 800.** Page titles, greetings, hero numbers, the wordmark.
- Body and UI: **Inter**, 400 to 700. Sentence case everywhere; no all-caps labels.

## App icon
The ring alone, cream ring and tan dot on an emerald (#0B2B24) tile. One icon for both modes.

## Rejected (so they are not re-proposed)
Logo: gritty stencil, bold athletic italic, lowercase Space Grotesk wordmark, open square,
typing cursor, step bars, open "u", the name wrapped around a circle, ring variants B to F on
5 November (Luke liked F, the gap on top making a U, but kept the current mark). Colour: the
old forest on bone and mint on near-black (replaced 5 November), Champagne, Storm, Fog (runner
ups), and the rest of the 132 combos.
