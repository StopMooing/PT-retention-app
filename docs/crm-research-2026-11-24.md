# What the best CRM apps do well (research for UNFNSHD, 24 Nov 2026)

**Purpose:** find what the top CRM apps across industries do well, so UNFNSHD's app and website can borrow the best of it.

**Method:**
- Mobbin, for how the apps and their websites actually look. The Mobbin screens and sections are listed at the end.
- Review sites and comparisons (Capterra, TechRadar, Zapier, Gymdesk, PT Distinction).
- What each product is widely known for.

The "does well" lines below are each product's best-known strength. They are not a hands-on test of all 100.

## The short version

Across all 100, the ones people love do the same five things:

1. **Open on "who needs you today".** The first screen is a short list of people to act on, never a database. Examples: HubSpot tasks, Pipedrive activities due, and the at-risk lists in Gainsight and ChurnZero.
2. **Say why someone is slipping.** A health score is backed by plain signals: no login, missed payment, a quiet contact. Planhat, Vitally and ChurnZero do this best.
3. **Turn a signal into the next action.** A playbook, reminder or ready-made message fires when something happens. Examples: ChurnZero playbooks, Gymdesk win-backs and failed-payment nudges, Keap automations, and Notion Mail's "remind me if no reply".
4. **Keep the whole relationship on one page.** Every touch (messages, meetings, payments) sits on one timeline per person. Attio, Mesh and HubSpot all do this.
5. **Stay easy.** "Ease of use" is the most praised strength in Capterra's CRM reviews. "Learning curve" and "price" are the two biggest complaints. The simple tools (Less Annoying CRM, Bigin) top the ease-of-use and value scores.

**Where UNFNSHD already matches the best:**
- "Needs you" with the reason each client is slipping.
- Reach out, with ready-made messages.
- Messaging a whole filtered group of clients.
- Client health.
- The weekly check-in and reply loop.

**What fitness and coaching software lacks:** Gymdesk's own comparison of seven gym platforms describes none of them as having at-risk client alerts. That is exactly UNFNSHD's angle. (Gymdesk wrote that article and ranks itself first, so treat it as a lead, not proof.)

## What to build next for coaches (ranked by retention impact)

1. **Follow-up reminders.** "Remind me in 3 days if they haven't replied" on every Reach out (Notion Mail, Amie catch-up reminders). This is small and very high value.
2. **A timeline per client.** Sessions, check-ins, messages, weigh-ins, program changes and swaps in one scroll (Attio, Mesh).
3. **Automatic playbooks the coach approves.** For example:
   - Missed 2 sessions → a drafted nudge waits in Needs you.
   - Check-in overdue 3 days → reminder.
   - A program ends in 7 days → "plan the next block".

   AI and automation draft; the coach always sends (as agreed in October).
4. **Retention numbers.** Average client lifespan, clients kept this month, and money kept by catching slipping clients. Fresha shows a returning-client rate on its home dashboard.
5. **Pause and win-back.** When a client wants to stop, offer a pause first, then a win-back message 30 days later (Churnkey, ProfitWell Retain, Gymdesk).
6. **Import clients.** From a spreadsheet, and from Trainerize or TrueCoach exports. Attio leads its footer with "Import from Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, CSV". Switching cost is the biggest barrier for the three PTs on Trainerize.
7. **Payments (Stripe).** Mindbody, PushPress and Keap all put billing next to the client. A failed payment is the strongest churn signal there is.

## What the best CRM websites do (for unfnshd.app)

- **One sentence on what it is and who it's for, above a real product screen.** Attio ("Customer relationship magic"), folk ("Finally, an intelligent CRM") and Twenty all lead with a big plain headline, a short line, one button and a real screenshot. None uses stock photos.
- **No-risk start.** HubSpot's "Get free CRM, no credit card required" sits right under the button.
- **Price in the unit the buyer thinks in.** Kit asks "How many email subscribers do you have?" with a slider. For coaches that becomes "How many clients do you coach?"
- **How it works in 3 steps.** Mailchimp uses "01 Connect, 02 Automate, 03 Know your people".
- **"It's all one system".** Beside shows a grid of 12 small items (icon, two words, one line), so the breadth is clear at a glance without a wall of text.
- **One section per kind of buyer.** HubSpot has tabs for sales leaders, salespeople, marketers and owners. For us that is coach and client.
- **A switching story.** Attio lists "Import from" the tools you already use.
- **An honest close.** A big final call to action, and a footer with Sign in.

**What we must not copy:** customer logos, user counts, star ratings and quotes. We have none yet, so the page uses a founder note from Luke instead.
