## Deadlift (Barbell) | Legs | barbell | strength | was Deadlift (Conventional) | aka conventional deadlift
how: Stand with the bar over the middle of your feet and grip it just outside your legs.
how: Drop your hips, flatten your back, brace, then push the floor away to stand up with the bar close to your legs.
how: Lower it by pushing your hips back, then bending your knees once it passes them.
