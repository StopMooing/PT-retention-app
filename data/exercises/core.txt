## Plank | Core | bodyweight | bodyweight
how: Rest on your forearms and toes with your elbows under your shoulders.
how: Hold your body in a straight line from head to heels.
how: Breathe steadily until the time is up.
cue: Squeeze your glutes and brace your stomach.
cue: Hips level, not high or sagging.
cue: Drop to your knees if your form slips.

## Weighted Plank | Core | plate | strength
how: Get into a forearm plank and have a partner place a plate across your upper back.
how: Hold a straight line from head to heels.
how: Have the plate removed before you come down.
cue: Plate sits on your upper back, not your lower back.
cue: Brace hard.
cue: Shorter holds, more weight.
