## Back Squat (Barbell) | Legs | barbell | strength | was Back Squat | aka back squat, squat
how: Set the bar on your upper back, feet about shoulder width and toes slightly out.
how: Brace, then sit down and back until your hips are at least level with your knees.
how: Drive up through your whole foot to stand tall.
cue: Big breath in and brace before each rep.
cue: Knees travel out over your toes.
cue: Chest up, push the floor away.
