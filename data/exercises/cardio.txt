## Treadmill Run | Cardio | machine | cardio
how: Start walking to warm up, then build to your running pace.
how: Run tall with relaxed arms and quick, light steps.
how: Walk for a few minutes at the end to cool down.
cue: Do not hold the handrails.
cue: Land under your hips, not out in front.
cue: Pick a pace you could talk at for easy runs.

## Treadmill Walk (Incline) | Cardio | machine | cardio
how: Set the treadmill to a steady walking speed.
how: Raise the incline until it feels like a solid uphill walk.
how: Walk for the set time without holding the rails.
cue: Hands off the rails.
cue: Tall posture.
cue: Breathe through your nose if you can.

## HIIT Treadmill | Cardio | machine | cardio
how: Warm up for 5 minutes.
how: Alternate fast running intervals with walking or easy jogging recovery.
how: Cool down with a few minutes of walking.
cue: Step onto the side rails to change speed safely.
cue: Hard means hard, easy means easy.
cue: Count your intervals.

## Sprints | Cardio | bodyweight | cardio
how: Warm up well with easy running and drills.
how: Sprint hard for the set distance.
how: Walk back and rest fully before the next one.
cue: Full rest between sprints.
cue: Drive your arms.
cue: Stay relaxed at top speed.

## Hill Sprint | Cardio | bodyweight | cardio
how: Find a steady hill and warm up well.

how: Work at the set intensity for the time or calories.

## Rowing Machine | Cardio | machine | cardio
how: Strap your feet in and hold the handle with straight arms.
how: Push with your legs, lean back slightly, then pull the handle to your lower ribs.
how: Return arms first, then lean forward, then bend your knees.
cue: Legs, body, arms. Then arms, body, legs.
cue: Most of the power comes from your legs.
cue: Smooth and long strokes.

## Ski Erg | Cardio | machine | cardio
